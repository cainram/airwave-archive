This renames and moves files in your library. Each recording is named from its episode's details, in a folder named after its series, like Gunsmoke/Gunsmoke - 1952-09-13 - Home Surgery.mp3. The files themselves aren't changed, and the app keeps track of them, so details, listening and feeds carry on. Other programs that point at the old names (another player's playlists, for example) will lose track of them. Every rename is written down, so you can put them back afterwards.
