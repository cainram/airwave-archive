New recordings wait here until you add them to your library. Check the details the app worked out for each one and fix anything that's wrong, then add it to your library (or reject files you don't want). An added file goes next to the other recordings of its series, or into a new folder named after the series. Nothing in your library is ever replaced.

Choose one or more audio files, or a zip of them, such as an export from another Airwave Archive (its episodes arrive with all their details). Video files (MKV, WebM, MP4), RealAudio, WMA, Ogg and Opus are fine too: the app converts them into files every player can play. Big files take a while: keep this page open until the upload finishes. (For a whole collection or a very big zip, copying it onto the server's inbox folder is faster.)
